Cookies (for private / login-only reels)

Facebook and Instagram hide most reels behind a login. Paste a cookies.txt export (or a raw Cookie: header) and rell will use it for every request. It stays on this machine only.

  1. Install the "Get cookies.txt LOCALLY" browser extension.
  2. Open facebook.com or instagram.com while logged in, click the extension, Export.
  3. Paste the file contents below, or upload the file.
